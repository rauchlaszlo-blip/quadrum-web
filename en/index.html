Manufacturing background

30 years of parquet manufacturing experience

Quadrum Parkett is Signum Parkett Kft.'s own parquet brand and direct sales website. We have manufactured engineered parquet for three decades. Until now we mainly produced finished and semi-finished parquet for Italian, Austrian and German partners, who marketed it under their own brands. We now also offer our own products directly to customers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the parquet suitable for underfloor heating?

Yes. Both two-layer and three-layer parquet are suitable for underfloor heating.

How long does manufacturing take?

Typically 30–60 days, depending on the selected product and pattern.

Which wood species are available?

Oak and ash are our standard choices. We can also manufacture the top layer from other commercially available and technically suitable wood species.

Which sizes are available?
Two-layer engineered planks Width: 120–140 mm Length: 500–1200 mm
Three-layer engineered planks Width: 120–250 mm Length: 400–2400 mm, maximum 2800 mm
Chevron Standard size: 120×600 and 120×1200 mm Custom sizes are also available
Panel parquet Standard size: 500×500 and 600×600 mm Custom sizes up to 1100×1100 mm, also available in rectangular form
How is delivery arranged?

On request, we can arrange transport with an external logistics partner. Collection using your own transport is also possible.

Who installs the parquet?

We can recommend a parquet installer on request.
